## 72. Buckling of Columns (Euler's Formula) / Втрата стійкості стрижнів (формула Ейлера)

### EN — Short
Buckling of Columns (Euler's Formula) explains how a material or structural element responds to a specified mechanical condition. It helps engineers predict behavior and avoid unreliable machine designs.

### EN — Detailed
- **Origin:** Leonhard Euler published elastic column buckling analysis in 1744.
- **Core idea:** Ideal elastic critical load Pcr = π²EI/(KL)²; K represents end restraint. Effective length reflects whether ends can rotate or translate. Real columns are never perfectly straight, so design rules reduce the ideal prediction.
- **Key components:** Identify applied load in N, dimensions in m, material properties in Pa or GPa, and the relevant cross-sectional geometry. Track stress in Pa or MPa, strain without units, and displacements in m or mm. Ideal elastic critical load Pcr = π²EI/(KL)²; K represents end restraint. Define the positive sign convention and check unit conversions before interpreting the result.
- **How to apply it:** Estimate effective length and compare critical load with design compression load. Start with a sketch showing restraints, load direction and dimensions. Select a representative section or specimen, calculate the ideal response, and compare it against the design requirement. Document assumptions, then check a second load case or a more detailed model where geometry is complex. For a prototype, measure the predicted response and compare it with calculations before raising the applied load.
- **Example:** For pinned steel column E = 200 GPa, I = 1 × 10⁻⁸ m⁴, L = 2 m, K = 1: Pcr = π² × 200 × 10⁹ × 10⁻⁸ / 2² = 4.93 kN. The numerical result applies to the stated geometry and assumptions; confirm that all converted areas and lengths use consistent SI units.
- **Limitations:** Not valid for stocky yielding columns, imperfections or eccentric loading without corrections; brace tall members. Real components may also contain manufacturing defects, residual stress, temperature gradients and changing service loads. Use relevant standards and a suitable design margin; inspect parts that could fail dangerously.
- **Related theories:** Tension and Compression (#65); Second Moment of Area (#70); Factor of Safety (#73)

### UA — Коротко
Тема «втрата стійкості стрижнів (формула ейлера)» пояснює поведінку матеріалів і деталей під дією навантажень. Вона потрібна для обґрунтованого вибору розмірів, матеріалів і безпечної роботи механізмів.
