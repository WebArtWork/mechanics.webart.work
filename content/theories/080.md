## 80. Thermal Expansion / Теплове розширення

### EN — Short
Thermal Expansion explains how a material or structural element responds to a specified mechanical condition. It helps engineers predict behavior and avoid unreliable machine designs.

### EN — Detailed
- **Origin:** Thermal expansion was studied experimentally in early modern physics and quantified in engineering tables.
- **Core idea:** For a uniform temperature change, ΔL = αL₀ΔT, with α in 1/K. A restrained member can develop force instead of visible extension. Different materials in one assembly can expand by unequal amounts.
- **Key components:** Identify applied load in N, dimensions in m, material properties in Pa or GPa, and the relevant cross-sectional geometry. Track stress in Pa or MPa, strain without units, and displacements in m or mm. For a uniform temperature change, ΔL = αL₀ΔT, with α in 1/K. Define the positive sign convention and check unit conversions before interpreting the result.
- **How to apply it:** Provide expansion gaps or sliding supports and calculate differential movement. Start with a sketch showing restraints, load direction and dimensions. Select a representative section or specimen, calculate the ideal response, and compare it against the design requirement. Document assumptions, then check a second load case or a more detailed model where geometry is complex. For a prototype, measure the predicted response and compare it with calculations before raising the applied load.
- **Example:** A 2 m steel rail with α = 12 × 10⁻⁶ 1/K warms by 50 K: ΔL = 12 × 10⁻⁶ × 2 × 50 = 0.0012 m = 1.2 mm. The numerical result applies to the stated geometry and assumptions; confirm that all converted areas and lengths use consistent SI units.
- **Limitations:** Temperature gradients and restraint produce thermal stresses; do not assume free expansion. Real components may also contain manufacturing defects, residual stress, temperature gradients and changing service loads. Use relevant standards and a suitable design margin; inspect parts that could fail dangerously.
- **Related theories:** Hooke's Law and Young's Modulus (#62); Creep and Temperature Effects (#76); Material Classes: Metals, Polymers, Ceramics and Composites (#78)

### UA — Коротко
Тема «теплове розширення» пояснює поведінку матеріалів і деталей під дією навантажень. Вона потрібна для обґрунтованого вибору розмірів, матеріалів і безпечної роботи механізмів.
