## 74. Stress Concentration / Концентрація напружень

### EN — Short
Stress Concentration explains how a material or structural element responds to a specified mechanical condition. It helps engineers predict behavior and avoid unreliable machine designs.

### EN — Detailed
- **Origin:** Kirsch analyzed stresses around a circular hole in 1898; later work extended notch factors.
- **Core idea:** Local peak stress is σmax = Ktσnom, where Kt depends on geometry. A smooth fillet reduces peak stress compared with a sharp shoulder. Nominal stress definitions must match the source of the concentration factor.
- **Key components:** Identify applied load in N, dimensions in m, material properties in Pa or GPa, and the relevant cross-sectional geometry. Track stress in Pa or MPa, strain without units, and displacements in m or mm. Local peak stress is σmax = Ktσnom, where Kt depends on geometry. Define the positive sign convention and check unit conversions before interpreting the result.
- **How to apply it:** Use charts or finite elements to assess holes, shoulders and fillets. Start with a sketch showing restraints, load direction and dimensions. Select a representative section or specimen, calculate the ideal response, and compare it against the design requirement. Document assumptions, then check a second load case or a more detailed model where geometry is complex. For a prototype, measure the predicted response and compare it with calculations before raising the applied load.
- **Example:** A plate with nominal tensile stress 40 MPa and geometric Kt = 2.5 has peak elastic stress σmax = 2.5 × 40 = 100 MPa. If yield is 250 MPa, local elastic yield ratio is 250/100 = 2.5. The numerical result applies to the stated geometry and assumptions; confirm that all converted areas and lengths use consistent SI units.
- **Limitations:** Plastic redistribution and fatigue sensitivity complicate use of a purely elastic Kt. Real components may also contain manufacturing defects, residual stress, temperature gradients and changing service loads. Use relevant standards and a suitable design margin; inspect parts that could fail dangerously.
- **Related theories:** Stress and Strain (#61); Factor of Safety (#73); Fatigue and the S–N Curve (#75)

### UA — Коротко
Тема «концентрація напружень» пояснює поведінку матеріалів і деталей під дією навантажень. Вона потрібна для обґрунтованого вибору розмірів, матеріалів і безпечної роботи механізмів.
