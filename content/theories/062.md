## 62. Hooke's Law and Young's Modulus / Закон Гука та модуль Юнга

### EN — Short
Hooke's Law and Young's Modulus explains how a material or structural element responds to a specified mechanical condition. It helps engineers predict behavior and avoid unreliable machine designs.

### EN — Detailed
- **Origin:** Robert Hooke published his elastic law in 1678; Thomas Young described the modulus in 1807.
- **Core idea:** In the linear elastic range σ = Eε and ΔL = FL/(AE); E is measured in Pa or GPa. A higher modulus means a stiffer material, not necessarily a stronger one. Use the actual effective length between load introduction points.
- **Key components:** Identify applied load in N, dimensions in m, material properties in Pa or GPa, and the relevant cross-sectional geometry. Track stress in Pa or MPa, strain without units, and displacements in m or mm. In the linear elastic range σ = Eε and ΔL = FL/(AE); E is measured in Pa or GPa. Define the positive sign convention and check unit conversions before interpreting the result.
- **How to apply it:** Choose E from material data and verify the operating stress remains elastic. Start with a sketch showing restraints, load direction and dimensions. Select a representative section or specimen, calculate the ideal response, and compare it against the design requirement. Document assumptions, then check a second load case or a more detailed model where geometry is complex. For a prototype, measure the predicted response and compare it with calculations before raising the applied load.
- **Example:** For steel E = 200 GPa, F = 10 kN, L = 2 m and A = 500 mm²: ΔL = 10,000 × 2 / (200 × 10⁹ × 0.0005) = 0.0002 m = 0.20 mm. The numerical result applies to the stated geometry and assumptions; confirm that all converted areas and lengths use consistent SI units.
- **Limitations:** Not valid beyond proportionality, for large strains or strongly nonlinear materials. Real components may also contain manufacturing defects, residual stress, temperature gradients and changing service loads. Use relevant standards and a suitable design margin; inspect parts that could fail dangerously.
- **Related theories:** Stress and Strain (#61); The Stress–Strain Curve (#63); Elastic and Plastic Deformation (#64)

### UA — Коротко
Тема «закон гука та модуль юнга» пояснює поведінку матеріалів і деталей під дією навантажень. Вона потрібна для обґрунтованого вибору розмірів, матеріалів і безпечної роботи механізмів.
